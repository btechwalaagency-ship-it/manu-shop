Laptop & Computer Repair — common questions

Is it worth repairing or should I replace it?

We’ll tell you honestly. Plenty of places will sell you a new machine when the old one needs a drive. If the repair doesn’t make financial sense we’ll say so — and we sell devices too, so either way we can help.

How much will it cost?

We diagnose the fault and quote you before any work starts. You approve the price first.

My laptop is really slow. Can that be fixed?

Almost always, and usually cheaply. A mechanical hard drive swapped for an SSD is the single biggest difference you can make to an older machine.
