Questions

Phone Repair — common questions

How long does a screen repair take?

Most screen replacements are done the same day, and often while you wait at the kiosk. If we need to order a part for a less common model we’ll tell you how long before you leave it with us.

Will I lose my photos?

A normal screen or battery repair doesn’t touch your data. If the phone is already dead or won’t boot we’ll talk to you about data recovery before doing anything.

Do you repair all brands?

iPhone, Samsung, Google Pixel, Oppo, Motorola and most other brands. If we can’t source a part for an unusual model we’ll say so rather than keep you waiting.

Do I need an appointment?

No — walk in during centre trading hours. Calling ahead on 0470 282 957 helps us have the part ready.
