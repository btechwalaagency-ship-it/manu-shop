CCTV Installation & Service — common questions

How many cameras do I need?

For most homes four covers the front door, back door, driveway and yard. We’ll walk the property and quote on what it actually needs rather than selling you a fixed package.

Can I watch the cameras from my phone?

Yes. Every system is set up on your phone before we leave.

How long is footage kept?

That depends on the recorder’s storage, the number of cameras and the quality. Two to four weeks is typical, and we size the storage to the retention you want.

Can you service a system someone else installed?

Yes — cameras that have dropped offline, recorders that have stopped recording, and analogue systems being upgraded to IP.
