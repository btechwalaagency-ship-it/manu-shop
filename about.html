About us

Your local repair kiosk in Beechboro

Phones, laptops and computers — sold, repaired and recovered, by the person who actually does the work.

Who we are

MM Repair Master is a repair and device kiosk inside The Spring Shopping Centre in Beechboro. Walk up, show us the problem, and in most cases you’ll have it back the same day.

We handle phones, laptops and computers — cracked screens, dead batteries, failed drives, viruses, and the photos you thought were gone for good. We also sell devices, build gaming PCs, and install CCTV for homes and small businesses around Perth.

Being in your local centre rather than online means you can see us face to face, before and after the repair. That tends to keep everyone honest.

How we work

Quote first

You know the price before any work starts. If a repair isn’t worth doing, we tell you.

Plain English

We explain what failed and why, without the jargon and without talking down to you.

Fix the cause

Not the symptom. A device that comes back next month helps nobody.

Where we work

The kiosk serves Beechboro and the surrounding north-eastern Perth suburbs — Morley, Bayswater, Noranda, Bennett Springs, Caversham, Lockridge, Kiara, Eden Hill, Ballajura and Malaga. On-call service and CCTV installation cover the wider Perth area.
